# Rigor checklist — adapted from `ara-rigor-reviewer`

Score each item: good / uncertain / needs action, link to hard evidence.

| Dimension | Question | Status / evidence |
|---|---|---|
| D1 Evidence relevance | Does metric measure the exact claim? cIoU vs per-image IoU? | Pending |
| D2 Falsifiability | Is an outcome specified that would show our idea failed? | Pending |
| D3 Scope | Are subset results clearly labeled? No image→video overgeneralization? | Pending |
| D4 Coherence | Did the improvement respond to an observed weakness? | Pending |
| D5 Exploration integrity | Were failed runs and pivots recorded before hindsight editing? | Pending |
| D6 Methodological rigor | Fixed splits/config, paired ablation, timing warmups, repetitions? | Pending |

**Level A rule:** completion ≠ a positive metric. Negative or inconclusive is valid with an honest report.
